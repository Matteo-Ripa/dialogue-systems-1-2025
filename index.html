Take over the state

Game Overview:
You are a character inspired by a street protest to overthrow the government.
Navigate through conversations and make critical decisions using only your voice.
Every choice impacts your path and the story's outcome.

How to Play:
Listen carefully to the narrative and options presented.
Respond with clear voice commands to make choices or give instructions.
Use strategic thinking and memory to progress through the story.
Immerse yourself in the protagonist's perspective to make authentic decisions.

Goal:
Lead a successful revolution by making the right choices and building alliances along the way.
Remember, your voice is your tool, and every decision counts.
